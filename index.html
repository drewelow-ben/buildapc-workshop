III. Resourcing Equipment: New vs. Used

When you plan to build your own PC, there is a choice to use new hardware or used/refurbished components (or a combination). Both choices can work. This section explains the main reasons to pick each, and a short checklist for testing used parts.


Quick answer

  • New parts — may be easier to use, may come with a warranty, and have the newest features.
  • Used parts — much cheaper, far more eco-friendly, and often have enough features to last for years into the future.

Why people pick new parts

  • They usually work right away.
  • Most new parts include a manufacturer warranty.
  • You get current features like faster speeds and newer ports.
  • Less time needed for testing and troubleshooting.
  • Easier to ensure compatibility

Why people pick used parts

  • Used parts are cheaper, so you can build systems on a much smaller budget.
  • Reusing parts helps the environment by keeping electronics out of landfills.
  • Local reuse groups and volunteers may donate parts.
  • Users get more experience with troubleshooting and repair skills.

Pros and cons at a glance

New parts

  • Pros: Reliable, warranty, up-to-date specs.
  • Cons: More expensive, higher manufacturing impact on the environment.

Used parts

  • Pros: Lower cost, eco-friendly, great for hands-on learning.
  • Cons: Often sold "as-is", may fail, may need cleaning or simple repairs.

Simple testing checklist for used parts

  1. Clean off dust and look for visible damage (cracks, burn marks, missing pins).
  2. Test the part in a working system.
  3. Check that power supplies are the right wattage and in good shape.
  4. Boot to BIOS or a basic OS to confirm the system starts.
  5. Run a memory test or quick disk check when possible.
  6. Donate broken or failed components to a local electronics recycling center.
  7. If a part smells like burned electronics, do not use it.

Where to find used parts and help

Look for donations or low-cost parts from local reuse centers, repair cafes, makerspaces, and community groups. You can also check online marketplaces but buy from sellers with good ratings.
